Howdy! I'm Miguel. I'm pursuing a bachelor's degree in Information Systems & Analytics at Louisiana State University and expect to graduate in May 2029. I'm interested in internships in IT infrastructure, cloud operations, and technical support.

## What I Work On

Outside class, I run a Linux homelab on Raspberry Pis. I use it to practice running services in Docker, configuring DNS and remote access, and troubleshooting connectivity. I also built and operated a Discord bot that gave me hands-on experience with Azure hosting, MongoDB, and supporting users. You can read more about the bot on my [Projects page](/projects/).

## How I Got Started

I grew up in Dallas watching my dad work on his custom-built computer. By seven, I was installing operating systems on my own, including a prerelease version of Windows 10.

![Miguel installing Windows on his computer at age seven](/images/young-me-computer.png)

I also spent much of middle and high school in choir, including jazz and show choir. I still appreciate how much performing with an ensemble taught me about working with other people.
